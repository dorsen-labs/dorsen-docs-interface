Dorsen Chain is a data oriented blockchain. Dorsen Chain offers several unique primitives:

- Lower gas fees than other EVM chains
- Faster transactions than other EVM chains
- EVM Equivalence for compatibility with other EVM chains


These primitives enable Dorsen Chain builders to build never before possible applications and reach a global audience.

Dorsen Chain is built on open source standards and open source infrastructure.
